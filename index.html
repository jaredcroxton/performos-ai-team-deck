Two Tiers, Same 10 Agents

Standard or Advanced
Pick Your Depth

Both tiers give you the same 10 customisable AI agents. Same tool connections. Same zero data retention. The difference is the model size and capability behind each agent.

Standard
Fast, Efficient, High Volume
8B parameter models
The "B" stands for billion parameters, which is the number of connections the model learned during training. 8 billion is a lot. These models handle everyday business tasks with speed and precision across all 10 agents at the same time.

Think of it like this: 8B models are highly competent generalists. They write well, analyse data, coach teams, review contracts, and research topics. They do all of it fast and they do it for all 10 agents simultaneously.

Best fit when your priority is:
• Processing volume across all agents
• Speed on routine and repeatable tasks
• Cost-efficient hardware (24GB RAM)

Same compliance as Advanced:
• Zero data retention ✓
• Full data compliance ✓
• 10 customisable agents ✓
Advanced
Deep, Powerful, Complex Reasoning
14B to 70B parameter models
Same as Standard but with significantly more processing capacity. A 70B model has nearly 9 times more learned connections than an 8B model. That translates to stronger reasoning, deeper analysis, and meaningfully better performance on ambiguous or high-stakes tasks.

Think of it like this: if 8B models are senior employees, 14B to 70B models are the partners. They handle everything the 8B models do, but they go deeper. Better at complex strategy, nuanced judgement, and work where getting it right matters more than getting it fast.

Hardware note: Advanced models need more memory during setup. We provision 48GB RAM during your onboarding to accommodate the larger model sizes. This is handled as part of the setup fee.

Same compliance as Standard:
• Zero data retention ✓
• Full data compliance ✓
• 10 customisable agents ✓
The simplest way to think about it: Standard is like having 10 highly efficient specialists. Advanced is like having 10 senior experts. Both get the job done. The experts just go deeper when the work demands it. Price is at the bottom.
